# Survivor 50 Tracker
## Design System – Dark Tribal Firelight

---

## CORE AESTHETIC

The UI should feel like:

- Tribal council at night
- Firelight glow against dark jungle
- Strategic tension
- Serious and immersive
- Premium and intentional

This is NOT a dashboard.
This is a strategy war board.

Avoid anything that feels corporate or generic.

---

## COLOR PALETTE

### Background
- Primary background: near-black charcoal
- Secondary background: deep brown-black
- Subtle gradient overlays allowed

Example token names:
- backgroundPrimary
- backgroundSecondary
- surfaceCard

---

### Firelight Accents
Used sparingly for glow and emphasis.

- Ember orange
- Burnt red
- Warm amber
- Deep gold

These are highlight colors, not primary UI colors.

Example token names:
- firePrimary
- fireGlow
- ember
- tribalGold

---

### Tribe Colors

These should feel rich and saturated but slightly darkened:

- tribeRed
- tribeBlue
- tribeYellow

Avoid neon tones.
Avoid bright default Tailwind colors.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

Tone: Dramatic but clean.

Headers:
- Slightly bold
- Tight letter spacing
- Strong presence

Body:
- Clean sans-serif
- High readability

Avoid:
- Playful fonts
- Rounded tech fonts
- Overly thin weights

---

## SPACING

Breathing room is important.

- Cards should not feel cramped.
- Grid should feel organized and deliberate.
- Avoid excessive padding.

Spacing should feel structured and intentional.

---

## CARD DESIGN (PLAYER CARDS)

Cards should feel:

- Slightly elevated
- Dark surface
- Soft glow on hover (subtle fire glow)
- Clean edges (not overly rounded)

Use:
- Custom border radius token (not full rounded-xl everywhere)
- Soft inner shadow or subtle border

---

## GLOW SYSTEM

Glow should be used:

- On hover
- On selected players
- On strong alliances (#1 alliance lines)

Glow color should match fire accent.

Glow must be subtle.
Never overpowering.

---

## ALLIANCE CONNECTIONS

Edges should be:

- Clean
- Color-coded by relationship type
- Slightly glowing
- No cartoon styling

Examples:
- Core Alliance → thicker warm gold line
- Alliance → thinner amber line
- Enemy → thin red dashed line

---

## INTERACTION FEEL

- No bouncy animations
- No playful transitions
- Subtle fade and glow only
- Smooth drag movement

The app should feel serious and strategic.

---

## WHAT TO AVOID

- Blue primary buttons
- Bright gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Overuse of blur
- White cards
- SaaS dashboard vibes

---
